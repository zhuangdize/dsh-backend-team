# Clarification

## Open Questions

Record questions that could change scope, behavior, security, or data ownership. Mark each question `Open`, `Answered`, or `Deferred` and include the decision owner.

## Assumptions

List assumptions made while refining the request, including the user role, deployment boundary, retention period, and compatibility expectations.

## Resolved Decisions

For every answered question, record the answer, evidence, and the affected requirement or acceptance criterion.
